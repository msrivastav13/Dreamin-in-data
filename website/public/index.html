Popular Questions

How do I track my order?

Once your order is confirmed, you can track it in real-time from the Pronto app or website. Go to My Orders and tap on the active order to see a live map with your driver's location, estimated arrival time, and order status updates.

You'll also receive push notifications at key stages: when the restaurant confirms your order, when the driver picks it up, and when they're approaching your location.

What is Pronto's refund policy?

We want every order to be perfect. If something goes wrong, here's how refunds work:

  • Missing items: Full refund for the missing item(s) plus applicable taxes and fees.
  • Wrong items: Full refund or re-delivery of the correct item at no extra charge.
  • Quality issues: Partial or full refund depending on the severity, assessed case-by-case.
  • Late delivery (30+ minutes past estimate): Service fee refund and a credit toward your next order.
  • Order never arrived: Full refund of the entire order amount.

Refund requests must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery. Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 3-5 business days.

How do I change my delivery address after placing an order?

You can update your delivery address if the restaurant hasn't started preparing your order yet. Go to My Orders, select the active order, and tap Edit Address.

If the restaurant has already started preparing your food, address changes may not be possible as it could affect delivery time and driver routing. In that case, contact our support team through the app and we'll do our best to accommodate the change.

Note: Changing your address to a location significantly farther away may result in an additional delivery fee.

How does Pronto's pricing work?

Pronto's pricing includes several components:

  • Menu prices: Set by the restaurant. Some items may differ from in-store pricing.
  • Delivery fee: Ranges from $0.99 to $7.99 based on distance, demand, and restaurant partnership tier.
  • Service fee: A percentage-based fee (typically 10-15%) that helps cover platform operations.
  • Small order fee: A $2.00 fee applies to orders under $15 to cover delivery costs.

Pronto+ members enjoy $0 delivery fees on orders over $20, reduced service fees, and no small order fees. Plans start at $9.99/month.

What is Pronto+ and how do I subscribe?

Pronto+ is our premium membership that saves you money on every order:

  • $0 delivery fee on orders over $20
  • Reduced service fees (5% instead of 10-15%)
  • No small order fees
  • Exclusive member-only promotions and early access to new restaurants
  • Priority customer support

Pronto+ costs $9.99/month or $96/year (save $24). You can subscribe from your account settings. New members get a free 30-day trial. Cancel anytime with no penalties.

How do I report a problem with my order?

To report an issue with your order:

  1. Open the Pronto app and go to My Orders
  2. Select the order with the issue
  3. Tap Help or Report a Problem
  4. Choose the issue type (missing items, wrong items, quality, etc.)
  5. Add details and photos if applicable
  6. Submit your report

Our team reviews reports within 2 hours during business hours. For urgent issues (food safety, allergic reactions), use the emergency contact option for immediate assistance.
