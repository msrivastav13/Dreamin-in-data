Account & Payments

Managing your account, payment methods, refunds, and security settings.

Account Management

How do I create a Pronto account?

Creating an account takes less than a minute:

  1. Download the Pronto app or visit pronto.com
  2. Tap "Sign Up"
  3. Enter your email, phone number, and create a password
  4. Verify your phone number with the SMS code
  5. Add your delivery address and payment method

You can also sign up using your Google, Apple, or Facebook account for faster onboarding. All sign-up methods give you the same account features.

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password:

  1. Go to the login screen and tap "Forgot Password"
  2. Enter the email associated with your account
  3. Check your email for a reset link (valid for 1 hour)
  4. Click the link and create a new password

Password requirements: minimum 8 characters, at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or try again.

If you signed up with Google/Apple/Facebook, you don't have a Pronto password — use the social login button instead.

How do I update my profile information?

To update your profile, go to Account > Profile Settings:

  • Name: Editable anytime
  • Email: Change requires verification of the new email address
  • Phone number: Change requires SMS verification of the new number
  • Profile photo: Optional, visible to drivers during delivery
  • Dietary preferences: Set allergies and preferences to get personalized recommendations
How do I delete my Pronto account?

We're sorry to see you go. To delete your account:

  1. Go to Account > Settings > Delete Account
  2. Select a reason (optional but helps us improve)
  3. Confirm by entering your password
  4. Your account enters a 30-day deactivation period

During the 30-day window, you can reactivate by simply logging back in. After 30 days, all personal data is permanently deleted per our privacy policy. This includes order history, saved addresses, and payment methods.

Note: Outstanding Pronto credits or active Pronto+ subscriptions should be used or cancelled before deletion. Unused credits are forfeited upon account deletion.

Payment Methods

What payment methods does Pronto accept?

Pronto accepts the following payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
  • Digital wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal
  • Pronto Credits: Earned through referrals, promotions, and refunds
  • Gift cards: Pronto gift cards purchased online or at retail partners
  • Cash on delivery: Available in select markets (driver carries change for up to $50)

You can save multiple payment methods and set a default. Split payment across two methods is available for orders over $30.

How do I add or remove a payment method?

To manage payment methods:

  1. Go to Account > Payment Methods
  2. Tap "Add Payment Method" to add a new card or connect a digital wallet
  3. To remove, swipe left on the method and tap "Remove"
  4. To set a default, tap the method and select "Set as Default"

All card information is encrypted and stored securely through our PCI-compliant payment processor. Pronto never has access to your full card number.

You cannot remove a payment method if it's the only one on file and you have an active Pronto+ subscription. Add an alternative method first.

Why was I charged a different amount than expected?

Your final charge may differ from the estimate shown at checkout for several reasons:

  • Weight-based items: Items priced by weight (deli, produce) are charged at actual weight
  • Substitutions: If you allowed substitutions and a replacement item costs more
  • Tip adjustment: If you modified your tip after delivery
  • Tax calculation: Final tax may differ slightly from estimate based on actual items delivered
  • Authorization hold: Your bank may show a temporary hold for a slightly higher amount — this is released within 3-5 days

View a detailed receipt for any order in My Orders > Order Details > View Receipt.

Refunds & Credits

How long do refunds take to process?

Refund processing times depend on the refund method:

  • Pronto Credits: Instant. Available immediately for your next order.
  • Credit/debit card: 3-5 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.
  • PayPal: 1-3 business days.
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay: 3-5 business days (same as underlying card).

When eligible for a refund, you'll often be given the choice between instant Pronto Credits (with a 10% bonus) or a refund to your original payment method. For example, a $10 refund can become $11 in Pronto Credits.

How do Pronto Credits work?

Pronto Credits are a wallet balance you can use toward any order:

  • Earning credits: Referrals ($10), compensation for issues, promotional rewards, loyalty program redemptions
  • Using credits: Automatically applied to your next order (or toggle off in payment settings)
  • Expiration: Credits expire 90 days after being issued
  • Limitations: Cannot be transferred to another account or redeemed for cash

Check your credit balance anytime in Account > Pronto Credits. You'll see each credit's source and expiration date.

I was charged but my order was cancelled. Where's my refund?

If your order was cancelled (by you, the restaurant, or the system), the refund is processed immediately. However, it may take time to appear:

  • The charge you see may be an authorization hold, not an actual charge. Holds are released by your bank within 1-5 business days.
  • If it's an actual charge, the refund will appear in 3-5 business days.

Check My Orders > [Cancelled Order] > Refund Status for real-time tracking of your refund. If it's been more than 7 business days, contact support with your order number and we'll investigate with the payment processor.

Security & Privacy

How does Pronto protect my personal data?

Pronto takes data protection seriously:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Payment security: PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. We never store full card numbers.
  • Phone masking: Your real phone number is never shared with drivers. We use masked relay numbers.
  • Location data: Used only during active deliveries. Not sold to third parties.
  • Data minimization: We only collect what's necessary to provide the service.

Review our full privacy policy and download your data anytime from Account > Privacy > Download My Data.

How do I enable two-factor authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security:

  1. Go to Account > Security > Two-Factor Authentication
  2. Choose your 2FA method: SMS code or authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy)
  3. Follow the setup prompts to verify
  4. Save backup codes in a secure location

Once enabled, you'll need both your password and a verification code to log in on new devices. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA, especially if you have saved payment methods.

I think my account has been compromised. What do I do?

If you suspect unauthorized access:

  1. Immediately: Change your password from a trusted device
  2. Review: Check My Orders for any unfamiliar orders
  3. Remove: Delete any unrecognized payment methods
  4. Report: Contact support through Account > Security > Report Compromised Account
  5. Enable: Turn on 2FA if not already active

Our security team will investigate within 24 hours. If fraudulent orders were placed, you won't be charged. We may temporarily lock your account during investigation to prevent further unauthorized activity.

Pronto+ Membership

How do I cancel my Pronto+ subscription?

To cancel Pronto+:

  1. Go to Account > Pronto+ > Manage Subscription
  2. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
  3. Select a reason (optional)
  4. Confirm cancellation

Your benefits continue until the end of your current billing period. You won't be charged again. You can resubscribe anytime, but the free trial is only available once.

If you cancel within the first 48 hours of a paid billing cycle, you're eligible for a full refund of that month's charge. After 48 hours, no partial refunds are issued.

Can I share my Pronto+ membership with family?

Yes! Pronto+ Family allows you to share benefits with up to 4 additional household members:

  • Pronto+ Individual: $9.99/month — benefits for one account
  • Pronto+ Family: $14.99/month — benefits for up to 5 accounts at the same address

Family members get the same delivery and fee benefits but maintain separate order histories and payment methods. The primary account holder manages billing and can add/remove members anytime.

To upgrade: Account > Pronto+ > Upgrade to Family Plan.
