Delivery

Tracking orders, delivery times, driver communication, and delivery options.

Tracking & Timing

How do I track my delivery in real-time?

Once a driver is assigned to your order, you'll see a live tracking map in the app showing:

  • The driver's real-time location
  • Estimated time of arrival (ETA), updated dynamically
  • Order status: Picking up > On the way > Arriving
  • The route the driver is taking

You'll also receive push notifications at key moments: driver assigned, food picked up, driver nearby (2 minutes away), and delivered.

How are delivery times estimated?

Pronto's delivery time estimates are calculated using multiple factors:

  • Restaurant prep time: Based on the restaurant's historical data and current order volume
  • Distance: Straight-line and actual road distance to your address
  • Traffic conditions: Real-time traffic data for accurate routing
  • Driver availability: Current supply of drivers in the area
  • Weather: Adjusted for rain, snow, or extreme conditions

Average delivery time is 25-45 minutes. During peak hours (11:30am-1:30pm and 5:30pm-8:30pm), times may be 10-15 minutes longer.

My delivery is late. What can I do?

If your delivery exceeds the estimated time by more than 15 minutes, you can:

  1. Check the live tracker for the driver's current status
  2. Contact the driver directly through the in-app chat or call button
  3. If the order is 30+ minutes late, you'll automatically receive a service fee refund
  4. Contact support for further assistance if needed

Common reasons for delays include traffic congestion, restaurant prep time exceeding estimates, or difficulty finding your exact location. Adding clear delivery instructions helps prevent the last issue.

What are Pronto's delivery hours?

Pronto's platform is available 24/7, but actual delivery availability depends on restaurant hours in your area. Most restaurants are available:

  • Breakfast: 7:00 AM - 10:30 AM
  • Lunch: 11:00 AM - 2:30 PM
  • Dinner: 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM
  • Late night: 10:00 PM - 2:00 AM (limited selection)

Use the app to see which restaurants are currently open and accepting orders in your area.

Delivery Options

What delivery options are available?

Pronto offers several delivery options:

  • Standard delivery: Delivered in 25-45 minutes. Fee: $0.99-$7.99 based on distance.
  • Priority delivery: Bumps your order to the front of the queue. 15-30 minutes. Additional $2.99 fee.
  • Scheduled delivery: Choose a 30-minute window up to 7 days in advance. Standard delivery fee applies.
  • Group order: Multiple people add items; one delivery. Available for teams and families.

Availability of Priority delivery depends on driver supply and restaurant capacity at the time of ordering.

Can I get contactless delivery?

Yes! Contactless delivery is available for all orders. At checkout, select "Leave at door" as your delivery preference. The driver will:

  1. Place your order at your door or specified location
  2. Take a photo as proof of delivery
  3. Notify you through the app that your food has arrived

You can add specific instructions like "Leave on the porch table" or "Place by the garage door" in the delivery notes field.

What is Pronto's delivery radius?

Pronto delivers within a maximum radius of 8 miles (13 km) from each restaurant. However, the typical delivery zone is 1-5 miles, which ensures food arrives fresh and hot.

When you enter your delivery address, you'll only see restaurants within deliverable range. If your favorite restaurant isn't showing up, it may be slightly outside the delivery zone from your location.

Delivery fees scale with distance: shorter distances typically mean lower fees ($0.99-$2.99), while longer distances cost more ($4.99-$7.99).

Driver Communication

How do I contact my delivery driver?

Once a driver is assigned to your order, you can contact them through:

  • In-app chat: Send text messages directly. Best for delivery instructions or updates.
  • Phone call: Tap the phone icon on the tracking screen. Your number is masked for privacy.

Communication is available from the moment a driver accepts your order until 30 minutes after delivery. After that, you'll need to contact Pronto support for any issues.

How do I add delivery instructions?

Good delivery instructions help drivers find you faster. Add them in two places:

  • Address notes (saved): Go to your saved addresses and add permanent notes like "Building B, use side entrance, buzz #304"
  • Order-level notes: At checkout, add one-time instructions specific to this delivery

Helpful instructions include: gate codes, apartment/suite numbers, landmark descriptions, preferred drop-off spots, and whether to ring the doorbell or knock.

How do I tip my driver?

You can tip your driver at checkout or after delivery:

  • At checkout: Choose a suggested percentage (15%, 20%, 25%) or enter a custom amount
  • After delivery: You have 2 hours to add or modify your tip based on the delivery experience

100% of tips go directly to the driver. Tips are not factored into Pronto's delivery fee calculations. The default tip suggestion is 18% of your subtotal.

Drivers see the tip amount only after completing the delivery, so tipping does not affect service quality for the current order. However, consistently good tips help attract drivers to your area for future orders.

Delivery Issues

My order was marked delivered but I didn't receive it

This is rare but we take it seriously. Steps to resolve:

  1. Check the delivery photo (if contactless) — it may be at a neighbor's door or alternate location
  2. Check with neighbors, front desk, or building security
  3. Review the delivery instructions to see if an alternate drop-off was specified
  4. If still not found, report immediately through My Orders > Help > Order Not Received

We investigate using GPS data and delivery photos. If the order was not delivered to you, you'll receive a full refund. Reports must be made within 4 hours of the marked delivery time.

The driver can't find my location. What should I do?

If a driver is having trouble finding you:

  1. Check the in-app chat — the driver may have sent a message
  2. Call the driver using the phone button on the tracking screen
  3. Share your exact GPS location using the "Share Location" button
  4. Go to a visible landmark if possible and let the driver know

To prevent this in the future, add detailed delivery instructions to your saved address including building color, nearby landmarks, and specific entry points.

Can I change the delivery address after the driver picks up?

Address changes after driver pickup are limited because they affect routing and delivery time. However:

  • Minor changes (different unit in same building, next door): Contact the driver directly through chat
  • Moderate changes (same neighborhood, within 1 mile): Contact support — may be possible with a small fee adjustment
  • Major changes (different neighborhood/area): Generally not possible after pickup. The order will be delivered to the original address.
