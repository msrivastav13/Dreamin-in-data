Merchants

Information for restaurant partners: onboarding, menu management, orders, and payouts.

Getting Started

How do I partner with Pronto as a restaurant?

Joining Pronto as a restaurant partner is straightforward:

  1. Visit pronto.com/merchants and click "Get Started"
  2. Fill out your business details: name, address, cuisine type, hours
  3. Upload required documents: business license, food safety certification, tax ID
  4. Choose your partnership plan (see commission tiers below)
  5. Set up your menu using our Merchant Portal or menu import tool
  6. Complete a brief onboarding session with your account manager

The full onboarding process typically takes 3-5 business days from application to going live. We'll provide a tablet for order management at no extra cost.

What are Pronto's commission rates for merchants?

Pronto offers three partnership tiers:

  • Basic (15% commission): Delivery and ordering platform access. Your restaurant appears in standard search results.
  • Plus (25% commission): Everything in Basic plus featured placement, access to promotions engine, branded storefront, and priority driver assignment.
  • Premium (30% commission): Everything in Plus, plus homepage carousel placement, dedicated account manager, marketing campaigns, and analytics dashboard.

Commission is calculated on the food subtotal (before taxes, fees, and tips). You can switch tiers monthly. New merchants get the first month at the Basic rate regardless of chosen tier.

What equipment does Pronto provide?

All merchant partners receive:

  • Pronto tablet: Dedicated device for receiving and managing orders (free loan, returned upon partnership end)
  • Thermal printer: For printing order receipts and labels
  • Branded packaging: Initial supply of bags and stickers (reorder at cost)
  • Training materials: Staff quick-reference guides and video tutorials

Plus and Premium tier merchants also receive a countertop stand, dedicated phone line, and custom signage for their storefront.

Menu Management

How do I update my menu on Pronto?

You can update your menu anytime through the Merchant Portal:

  1. Log into merchants.pronto.com
  2. Go to Menu Management
  3. Add, edit, or remove items as needed
  4. Set prices, descriptions, and photos
  5. Organize items into categories
  6. Click "Publish" to make changes live

Changes go live within 5 minutes of publishing. You can also schedule menu changes in advance (e.g., switch to a lunch menu at 11 AM automatically).

How do I mark items as sold out?

There are two ways to mark items unavailable:

  • From the tablet: Tap the item on your active menu and select "Mark Sold Out." Choose to make it unavailable for the rest of the day or until you manually restock.
  • From the Merchant Portal: Go to Menu Management, find the item, and toggle availability off.

Sold-out items appear grayed out to customers with a "Currently Unavailable" label. Items marked sold out for the day automatically become available again when your next operating day starts.

Can I set different prices on Pronto vs in-store?

Yes, you're free to set different pricing on Pronto. Many merchants add a 10-20% markup to offset commission costs. However, keep in mind:

  • Pronto displays a notice to customers if prices differ significantly from in-store pricing
  • Competitive pricing leads to higher order volume and better search ranking
  • Pronto+ partner restaurants that maintain in-store pricing receive bonus visibility

Set your pricing strategy in Merchant Portal > Settings > Pricing Strategy.

How do I add photos to my menu items?

High-quality photos increase order rates by up to 30%. To add photos:

  1. Go to Menu Management in the Merchant Portal
  2. Select an item and click "Add Photo"
  3. Upload an image (minimum 1200x800px, JPEG or PNG)
  4. Crop and adjust using our built-in editor

Photo guidelines: natural lighting, clean background, accurate representation of portion size. Premium tier merchants get a free professional photo shoot of up to 20 items during onboarding.

Orders & Operations

How do I manage incoming orders?

Orders arrive on your Pronto tablet with an audible alert. For each order you can:

  • Accept: Confirm the order and set estimated prep time (auto-accepted after 3 minutes if not declined)
  • Decline: Reject with a reason (out of items, too busy, closing soon). Frequent declines affect your search ranking.
  • Adjust prep time: Update the estimate if kitchen is busier than expected
  • Mark ready: Signal that the order is packed and ready for driver pickup

You can also integrate with your existing POS system. Pronto supports integrations with Square, Toast, Clover, and Aloha. Contact your account manager for setup.

How do I pause accepting orders?

If your kitchen is overwhelmed or you need a break, you can temporarily pause orders:

  • Busy mode (30/60/90 minutes): Stops new orders temporarily. Active orders still need to be fulfilled.
  • Close early: Marks you as closed for the rest of the day.
  • Item-specific pause: Disable specific items without going offline entirely.

Find these options under Store Status on your tablet or Merchant Portal. We recommend using Busy mode during unexpected rushes rather than declining individual orders, as it doesn't affect your reliability score.

What happens if I need to cancel an order?

If you need to cancel an accepted order (out of a key ingredient, equipment failure, etc.):

  1. Tap the order on your tablet and select "Cancel Order"
  2. Select a reason from the dropdown
  3. The customer will be notified immediately and refunded in full

Merchant-initiated cancellations are tracked. Maintaining a cancellation rate below 2% keeps your search ranking unaffected. Rates above 5% may result in temporary visibility reduction.

Payments & Analytics

How and when do I get paid?

Pronto processes merchant payouts on a weekly cycle:

  • Payout schedule: Every Monday for the previous week's orders (Monday-Sunday)
  • Payment method: Direct deposit to your registered bank account
  • Processing time: 1-2 business days after payout is initiated
  • Minimum payout: $25 (balances below this roll over to the next week)

You can view detailed payout breakdowns in Merchant Portal > Financials, including per-order revenue, commission deductions, adjustments, and taxes.

Premium tier merchants can opt for daily payouts for an additional 1% processing fee.

What analytics does Pronto provide to merchants?

The Merchant Portal includes comprehensive analytics:

  • Sales dashboard: Revenue, order count, average order value, growth trends
  • Menu performance: Best sellers, underperforming items, item-level profitability
  • Customer insights: New vs. returning customers, order frequency, peak times
  • Operational metrics: Prep time accuracy, cancellation rate, rating breakdown
  • Competitive benchmarks: How you compare to similar restaurants in your area (anonymized)

Plus and Premium tier merchants get access to advanced analytics including customer cohort analysis, promotional ROI, and demand forecasting.

How do chargebacks and disputes work?

When a customer reports an issue (missing item, wrong item, quality), here's how it's handled:

  • Customer-reported missing items: Refunded from Pronto's budget if driver error; deducted from merchant if item was never included in the bag.
  • Wrong items: Merchant responsibility. Deducted from next payout unless disputed.
  • Quality complaints: Reviewed case-by-case. Photos are shared with you for context.

You can dispute any chargeback within 48 hours through Financials > Disputes. Provide evidence (photos, timestamps) and our team will review within 3 business days.
