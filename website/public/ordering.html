Ordering

Everything about placing orders, customizing meals, promotions, and order issues.

Placing an Order

How do I place an order on Pronto?

Placing an order on Pronto is simple:

  1. Open the Pronto app or visit pronto.com
  2. Enter your delivery address to see available restaurants near you
  3. Browse restaurants or search for a specific cuisine or dish
  4. Select a restaurant and browse their menu
  5. Add items to your cart, customize as needed
  6. Review your cart, apply any promo codes
  7. Choose your payment method and confirm the order

Your order will be sent to the restaurant immediately. You'll receive an estimated delivery time once the restaurant confirms.

Can I schedule an order for later?

Yes! Pronto supports scheduled orders up to 7 days in advance. When placing your order, tap the delivery time at the top of the screen and select "Schedule for later." Choose your preferred date and 30-minute delivery window.

Scheduled orders are sent to the restaurant at the appropriate time to ensure freshness. You can modify or cancel a scheduled order up to 1 hour before the delivery window.

What is the minimum order amount?

Minimum order amounts vary by restaurant, typically ranging from $10 to $20. The minimum is displayed on the restaurant page before you start ordering.

If your cart total is below the minimum, you'll see how much more you need to add. Orders under $15 (regardless of restaurant minimum) will have a $2.00 small order fee applied, unless you're a Pronto+ member.

Can I order from multiple restaurants at once?

Currently, each order is placed with a single restaurant. If you'd like items from multiple restaurants, you'll need to place separate orders. Each order will have its own delivery fee and estimated arrival time.

Pronto+ members can use the "Group Cart" feature to combine orders from up to 3 restaurants within the same delivery zone into a single delivery, with just one delivery fee.

How do I customize items or add special instructions?

When adding an item to your cart, you'll see available customization options (size, toppings, sides, etc.) set by the restaurant. Select your preferences before adding to cart.

For special instructions (allergies, spice level, preparation preferences), use the "Special Instructions" text field available for each item. There's also an order-level note field at checkout for delivery-related instructions.

Important: While restaurants try their best to accommodate special requests, they cannot guarantee all modifications, especially during peak hours.

Modifying & Canceling Orders

Can I modify my order after placing it?

You can modify your order only if the restaurant hasn't started preparing it. Look for the "Modify Order" button on the order tracking screen. Once the status changes to "Preparing," modifications are no longer possible.

Modifications that increase the order total will charge the difference to your payment method. Reductions will be refunded as Pronto credit.

How do I cancel an order?

To cancel an order, go to My Orders, select the active order, and tap Cancel Order. Cancellation policies depend on timing:

  • Before restaurant confirms: Full refund, no fees
  • After restaurant confirms but before prep starts: Full refund minus a $2 cancellation fee
  • After preparation begins: Partial refund (50-75%) depending on preparation stage
  • After driver pickup: No refund available (contact support for exceptions)

Frequent cancellations after restaurant confirmation may result in a temporary restriction on your account.

What happens if a restaurant cancels my order?

If a restaurant cancels your order (due to being too busy, running out of ingredients, or closing early), you'll receive a full refund immediately plus a $5 Pronto credit for the inconvenience.

You'll be notified instantly and given the option to reorder from the same restaurant (if they're still accepting orders) or browse other nearby restaurants with a similar cuisine.

Promotions & Discounts

How do I apply a promo code?

At checkout, tap "Add Promo Code" below your order summary. Enter the code and tap "Apply." If valid, you'll see the discount reflected in your total immediately.

Promo codes may have restrictions: minimum order amounts, specific restaurants, first-time use only, or expiration dates. The full terms are shown when you apply the code.

What promotions does Pronto offer?

Pronto regularly offers several types of promotions:

  • New user discount: 50% off your first 3 orders (up to $15 off each)
  • Free delivery promotions: Select restaurants offer $0 delivery during off-peak hours
  • Bundle deals: Restaurant-specific combos at a discounted price
  • Referral rewards: Get $10 for each friend who places their first order
  • Weekly specials: Rotating discounts on partner restaurants every Monday
  • Loyalty rewards: Earn 1 point per $1 spent. Redeem 100 points for $5 off

Check the "Offers" tab in the app for currently active promotions personalized to your ordering history.

How does the referral program work?

Share your unique referral code with friends. When they sign up and complete their first order of $15 or more, you both benefit:

  • You get: $10 Pronto credit
  • They get: $10 off their first order

There's no limit to how many friends you can refer. Find your referral code in Account > Refer a Friend. Credits are applied automatically once your friend's first order is delivered.

Order Issues

My order is missing items. What do I do?

We're sorry about that. To report missing items:

  1. Go to My Orders and select the affected order
  2. Tap Help > Missing Items
  3. Select which items were missing
  4. Submit the report

You'll receive a refund for the missing items (including proportional tax and fees) within minutes. For orders missing more than half the items, you may also receive additional Pronto credit.

I received the wrong order. What should I do?

If you received someone else's order or completely wrong items, report it immediately through My Orders > Help > Wrong Order. You'll be offered:

  • A full refund to your original payment method, OR
  • A redelivery of the correct order at no extra charge (subject to restaurant availability)

For food safety reasons, please do not consume items you're unsure about, especially if you have allergies. You do not need to return the incorrect items.

My food arrived cold or damaged. Can I get a refund?

Yes. Quality issues like cold food, spilled items, or damaged packaging are valid grounds for a refund. Report the issue through My Orders > Help > Quality Issue and include photos if possible.

Depending on the severity:

  • Minor issues (slightly cold, packaging dented): Partial refund or Pronto credit
  • Major issues (spilled, inedible, safety concern): Full item refund
  • Entire order affected: Full order refund including fees
